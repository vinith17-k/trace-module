How TRACE works

When you contact NHAA (14566), the chatbot, IVRS, or the integrated portal, TRACE listens to what you share — through voice or text — and gently assesses your level of distress so the right support reaches you as fast as possible.

What TRACE looks for

Signs of fear, depression, suicidal thoughts, intimidation, and social isolation — never to judge you, only to understand how urgently you need help.

What happens next

Depending on what you've shared, we may connect you with a counsellor, legal aid, medical assistance, police intervention, or witness protection. You'll always be told what's happening and why.

Who's involved

Department of Social Justice and Empowerment, State Governments, District Administrations, mental health professionals, law enforcement, and rehabilitation authorities — all working from the same, secure case record.

Privacy Policy

Your identity is only ever visible to a counsellor assigned to your case, and only if you choose to share it. You may use TRACE anonymously and check on your case later using a reference ID.

What we store

Your message or voice recording, a derived distress score, and any recommended next step. Raw recordings are never shared beyond the analysis needed to support you.

Who can see what

Counsellors see cases assigned to them. Law enforcement only sees cases explicitly flagged for police intervention or witness protection. Every access is logged.

Your consent

You can withdraw consent at any point in the conversation, and choose not to continue at any time without penalty.

Terms of Service

TRACE is a free government service offered under NHAA and the Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment. It is a support and triage tool, not a replacement for professional medical, psychiatric, or legal advice.

Fair use

This service exists to support victims and complainants of caste-based atrocities. Please use it honestly and respectfully.

Availability

TRACE aims to be available 24/7 across chat, voice, IVRS, and the integrated portal, though occasional maintenance windows may apply.
